# Olive Room — Room Manual

The Olive Room (Camera Oliva) is on the first floor of Casa Sole. It is 20 m²,
with a queen-size bed (160x190), a private en-suite bathroom with a walk-in
shower, a writing desk and a window overlooking the inner courtyard. It is a
quiet room, best suited to couples or solo travellers.

## Wi-Fi

- Network: **CasaSole-Guest** — Password: **oliva2026**
- For video calls use the 5 GHz network **CasaSole-Guest-5G** (same password).
- If the connection drops, unplug the white router in the first-floor hallway
  for ten seconds and plug it back in.

## The room

- Bed: queen size with cotton linen; spare blankets are in the chest under the
  window. Two spare pillows are in the wardrobe.
- Desk: the lamp has a USB socket for charging your phone.
- Kettle and tea selection are on the desk. Instant coffee sachets are in the
  drawer; the coffee machine is only in the Suite Giardino.
- Hair dryer: in the bathroom drawer. Iron and ironing board: ask Marco, they
  are kept in the hallway cupboard.
- Blackout curtains: pull the cord on the left to close them fully.
- The radiator can be adjusted with the knob, but please stay at or below 21 °C.
  In summer the air conditioning remote is on the bedside table; set it between
  24 and 26 °C and keep the window closed while it is on.

## Bathroom

- Walk-in shower with a rain head. Hot water takes about 30 seconds to arrive.
- Complimentary toiletries (soap, shampoo, shower cap) are on the shelf.
- Please do not flush wipes or sanitary products: use the bin provided.
- Towels are changed every three nights; spares are in the wardrobe.

## Safety

- Safe: inside the wardrobe. Default code **0000** — press the red button
  inside the door to set a new four-digit code.
- Fire extinguisher: hallway next to the front door. First-aid kit: bathroom
  drawer. Emergency number in Italy: **112**.
- Please lock the window when you leave the room.

## Breakfast

- Breakfast is included and served on the veranda from 7:30 to 10:00.
- You will find coffee, cappuccino, tea, juice, fresh bread, croissants, yogurt,
  fruit and cold cuts. Eggs and pancakes on request.
- Tell Marco the evening before if you need gluten-free, lactose-free or vegan
  options.

## House rules (short version)

- Check-in: 15:00–20:00. Late check-in 20:00–23:00 on request (€15 surcharge,
  to be confirmed one day in advance).
- Check-out: by 10:30. Free luggage storage on the veranda until 14:00.
- Quiet hours: 23:00–7:00. No parties.
- No smoking indoors — smoking is allowed in the garden only.
- Small pets allowed on request (€10 per night), never left alone in the room.
- Extra guests must be agreed in advance (€20 per night).

## Parking

- One free parking space per unit at Via delle Rose 8, behind the white gate.
  Use the remote control on the desk and close the gate afterwards.
- Do not drive into central Florence: the historic centre is a restricted
  traffic zone (ZTL) and fines arrive months later. Use the Villa Costanza car
  park outside the ZTL and take tram T1.

## Check-out checklist

1. Leave the keys on the desk and close the door behind you.
2. Close the window, turn off the air conditioning and the lights.
3. Take your rubbish to the bins in the courtyard (organic = brown, plastic and
   metal = yellow, paper = blue, general waste = grey).
4. Empty the safe and log out of any streaming account on the TV.
5. Leave used towels in the bathroom — no need to strip the bed.

For an early check-out (before 7:00) let Marco know the evening before: a
take-away breakfast box will be prepared for you.
